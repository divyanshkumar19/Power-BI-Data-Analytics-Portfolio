# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard

> An interactive Power BI dashboard built to transform 15,000 e-commerce transactions into meaningful business insights for sales, profitability, products, regions, and customer payment behavior.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **15,000 e-commerce transactions** using **Power BI**.

The raw dataset contains information about orders, products, categories, sales, profit, discounts, states, quantities, and payment methods.

The main objective of this project is to convert raw transactional data into an **interactive business intelligence dashboard** that allows users to quickly understand overall sales performance and identify important business trends.

Instead of manually analyzing thousands of rows, users can use the dashboard to explore key metrics and filter the data to investigate specific areas of the business.

---

## 🎯 Why This Dashboard Was Created

E-commerce businesses generate large amounts of transactional data every day. However, raw data alone does not provide an easy way to understand business performance.

This dashboard was created to answer important business questions such as:

- What are the total sales and profit?
- How many orders have been placed?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which product categories perform best?
- Which states contribute the most sales?
- How do sales change month by month?
- Which payment methods are most commonly used?
- What is the profit margin of each product category?
- How much quantity has been sold?
- What is the average discount given to customers?

The dashboard brings these answers together in a single interactive report.

# 💡 Key Insights

Based on the analysis:

- The dataset contains **15,000 orders**.
- Total sales are approximately **₹49.38M**.
- Total profit is approximately **₹7.38M**.
- More than **45,000 units** were sold.
- The average discount was approximately **15%**.
- **Electronics** generated the highest sales among the product categories.
- **Tablet** was the highest-selling individual product.
- **Rajasthan** recorded the highest sales among the states shown in the dashboard.
- **July** recorded the highest monthly sales.
- Payment mode analysis shows how customers distribute their purchases across different payment channels.
- Profit margins across the major product categories are relatively close, allowing category profitability to be compared directly.
