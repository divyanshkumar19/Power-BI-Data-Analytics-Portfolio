#  Zomato Food Delivery Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze Zomato food delivery data and uncover insights into sales performance, customer behavior, food preferences, payment methods, delivery status, restaurant types, and transaction trends.

---

## Project Overview

The **Zomato Food Delivery Analytics Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of food delivery operations and customer purchasing behavior.

The dashboard transforms raw food delivery data into interactive visualizations and KPIs, helping users understand **transaction trends, food demand, customer membership behavior, payment preferences, and delivery performance**.

The project demonstrates practical skills in **Power BI, Power Query, DAX, data modeling, data visualization, and business analysis**.

---

## Business Problem

Food delivery platforms generate large amounts of transactional and customer data. However, raw data alone makes it difficult to quickly understand:

* How sales and transactions change over time
* Which food items have the highest demand
* Which payment methods customers prefer
* How delivery status affects transactions
* How different customer membership types behave
* Which restaurant types contribute to transactions
* How customer and food categories interact with each other

The objective of this dashboard is to convert this data into an **interactive business intelligence solution** that makes these insights easier to explore and understand.

---

## Goal of the Dashboard

The primary objectives of this dashboard are to:

## Data Model

The dashboard uses multiple tables to organize and analyze the data:

### 1. Sales Data

Contains transactional information used for analyzing:

* Transactions
* Quantity
* Order dates
* Delivery status
* Payment methods

### 2. Customer Details

Contains customer-related information, including:

* Customer membership type
* Customer segmentation

### 3. Food Details

Contains food-related information, including:

* Food items
* Food types/categories

### 4. Restaurant Details

Contains restaurant-related information, including:

* Restaurant type

These tables work together through the Power BI data model to enable cross-filtering and interactive analysis.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard provides high-level KPIs for:

* **Total Quantity**
* **Total Transactions**
* **Average Quantity**

These KPIs provide a quick overview of overall food delivery performance.

---

### 📈 Monthly Quantity Analysis

A column chart shows **total quantity by month**, helping identify changes in food demand over time and highlight periods of higher or lower activity.

---

### Food Type vs Customer Membership

A matrix visual compares **food types against customer membership types** based on transaction volume.

This helps identify relationships between:

* Food preferences
* Customer membership
* Transaction behavior

---

### Delivery Status Analysis

A donut chart analyzes transactions based on **delivery status**.

This provides an overview of different delivery outcomes and helps evaluate delivery performance.

---

### Payment Method Analysis

A funnel chart compares transactions across different **payment methods**.

This helps identify customer payment preferences and understand how transactions are distributed across payment channels.

---

### Food-wise Quantity Analysis

A bar chart ranks food items based on **total quantity ordered**.

This allows users to identify high-demand food items and understand customer purchasing preferences.

---

### Transaction Trend Analysis

A line chart analyzes **transactions across years and months**, allowing users to identify:

* Growth or decline in transactions
* Seasonal patterns
* Monthly fluctuations
* Long-term transaction trends

---
