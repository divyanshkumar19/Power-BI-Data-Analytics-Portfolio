# Retail Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Retail Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard** built using Microsoft Power BI to help a retail company understand its sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and operational costs.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Retail companies generate large amounts of transactional data containing information about orders, customers, products, sales, discounts, shipping, regions, and profit.

However, raw transaction data does not directly provide clear answers to important business questions such as:

- Which regions generate the highest sales?
- Which products are the best sellers?
- Which customer segments generate the most profit?
- How are sales changing over time?
- How much discount is being given?
- How much is being spent on shipping?
- Which product categories contribute most to revenue?

To solve this problem, I developed an interactive **Retail Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard** using Power BI.

The dashboard transforms raw retail transaction data into meaningful KPIs, visualizations, and business insights.

# ❗ Business Problem

The company has a large amount of retail transaction data, but analyzing individual transactions manually makes it difficult to understand overall business performance.

The main challenges are:

1. Sales performance is difficult to monitor from thousands of transactions.
2. Regional sales performance is not immediately visible.
3. Identifying top-performing products requires manual analysis.
4. Profitability across customer segments is difficult to compare.
5. Discounts and shipping costs need to be monitored because they affect business performance.
6. Monthly sales trends are difficult to identify from raw transaction records.
7. Management needs an easy way to filter and analyze business performance.

### Business Objective

The objective of this project is to create a centralized interactive dashboard that converts raw retail transaction data into useful business information and supports **data-driven decision-making**.

---

# 💡 Solution Provided

To solve these problems, I cleaned and transformed the retail transaction data and developed an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard provides:

- Overall sales performance
- Profit analysis
- Order analysis
- Discount analysis
- Shipping cost analysis
- Regional sales analysis
- Product performance analysis
- Product category analysis
- Customer segment profitability
- Order priority analysis
- Monthly sales trends

All major business metrics are available in a single dashboard.

---

# 📊 Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard provides the following key performance indicators:

### Total Sales
Shows the overall revenue generated by the company.

### Total Profit
Shows the total profit generated from sales.

### Total Orders
Shows the number of orders placed.

### Average Discount
Shows the average discount provided on orders.

### Total Shipping Cost
Shows the total cost incurred for shipping orders.

These KPIs give management a quick overview of the company's overall performance.

# 📈 Dashboard Analysis

## 1. Sales by Month

The monthly sales chart shows how sales change throughout the year.

This helps the company:

- Identify high-sales months
- Identify low-sales months
- Understand sales patterns
- Support sales planning

---

## 2. Sales by Region

The dashboard compares sales across different regions.

This helps management understand:

- Which regions generate more revenue
- Regional sales differences
- Where additional business attention may be required

---

## 3. Top 10 Products by Sales

The dashboard identifies the top 10 products based on sales.

This helps the company:

- Identify high-demand products
- Understand product performance
- Support inventory planning
- Focus on important products

---

## 4. Sales by Product Category

Sales are analyzed across major product categories such as:

- Technology
- Furniture
- Office Supplies
- Consumer Products

This allows management to compare the contribution of different product categories to total sales.

---

## 5. Profit by Customer Segment

The dashboard analyzes profit across different customer segments:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office
- Small Business

This helps the company understand which customer groups contribute to profitability.

---

## 6. Sales by Order Priority

Sales are analyzed based on order priority:

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low
- Not Specified

This provides an overview of sales distribution across different order priorities.

# 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard contains interactive filters that allow users to analyze specific parts of the business.

Available filters include:

- Region
- Product Category
- Customer Segment
- Order Priority
- Order Date
- Payment Method
- Ship Mode

For example, a user can select a particular region and immediately see how its sales, products, customer segments, and other metrics change.
